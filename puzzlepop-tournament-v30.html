🏆 How to join the tournament

Open the game every day and collect your daily bonus — 7 days in a row — to unlock the tournament. Miss a day and your streak restarts. Once you're in, you stay in. Then turn Ranked ON before you start a Classic game, and your scores will count on the weekly leaderboard. Every week, the top 50 players win real money prizes, paid in crypto.
