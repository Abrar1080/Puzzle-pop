Privacy Policy — PuzzlePop

Last updated: 31 October 2026.

PuzzlePop is a block puzzle game with a weekly tournament. This policy explains what information the game collects and how it is used.

Information we collect

  • Account information: when you sign up or sign in, we store your email address and the username you choose. If you sign in with Google, Google shares only your name, email address and profile picture, as you approve on Google's sign-in screen.
  • Game data: your level progress, coins, streak, ranked game scores, daily spin results and leaderboard position, so the game, the weekly leaderboard and prizes can work.
  • Prize claims (winners only): if you finish in the weekly top 300 and choose to claim a prize, you enter a crypto wallet address and coin/network. We use it only to pay that prize. If you never claim, we never ask for it.
  • On your device only: guest play, theme and sound settings are stored on your own device and are not sent to us.

How your information is used

Your account and game data are used only to run the game: sign-in, saving progress, the weekly leaderboard, spin rewards and prize claims. We do not sell your personal information.

Where your data is stored

Account and game data are stored with our backend provider, Supabase, which protects each player's data so players can only see and change their own rows. Leaderboard names and scores are visible to other players by design — that is how the tournament board works.

Advertising

The Android apps show advertising from Google AdMob: a rewarded ad when you claim a daily bonus, a scratch-card repeat or an ad spin, and a full-screen ad after every fifth finished game. AdMob may collect device and advertising identifiers to show and measure those ads. The web version of the game shows no advertising. If the advertising provider or the ad placements change, this policy will be updated before the new version is released.

Children

The weekly tournament pays real prizes, so tournament accounts are for players who can legally receive a prize payout. Parents or guardians should supervise younger players.

Deleting your account

To delete your account and game data, contact us at the email address below and we will delete it. Deleting your account also removes you from the leaderboard and any unclaimed prize.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted here with a new date.
