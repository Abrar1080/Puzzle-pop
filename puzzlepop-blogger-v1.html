🎮 How to Play

  • Drag a piece onto the board — or tap a piece, then tap the board.
  • Fill a full row or column to pop it and score. Chain pops to build your combo multiplier.
  • Use Undo, Hint, Hammer (×2) and Bomb (×1) when you get stuck.
  • Three modes: Classic, 12 Levels with stars, and a new Daily Challenge every day.

🧩 About PuzzlePop

PuzzlePop is a free block puzzle game. No login, no account, no download — just open the page and play. Your best score is saved only on your own device.
